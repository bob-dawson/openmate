OpenMate is a native Android client for a self-hosted opencode server. It lets you follow and drive your coding agent from your phone instead of staying at your desk.

Features:

* Browse workspaces and sessions, and read or reply to messages.
* Incremental, sequence-based sync from opencode's own database into a local copy on your device, so you can read and reply to sessions offline.
* Connect over your local network, or automatically through a cloud relay so you can reach your agent from anywhere.
* Review file diffs, browse files, and answer permission and question prompts.
* Works with opencode v2.

OpenMate is not a hosted service and does not require any account. It talks only to the opencode server (and optional relay) that you configure. A small companion Bridge agent, available for Windows, Linux and macOS, runs next to opencode and provides the sync, pairing and relay features.
